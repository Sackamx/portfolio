## My scope

I designed graphics for running the Akademia Kształcenia Kadr profile: posts introducing the brand, expert carousels, course announcements and stories, as well as profile covers, recruitment banners, website banners and e-mail footers.

## Channels and goals

### Organic

Introducing the brand, carousels with practical knowledge for accountants and HR specialists, course and training announcements, a series about AI and stories with reminders and giveaways.

### Profiles and web

Facebook and LinkedIn covers, a recruitment campaign, and a web banner and e-mail footer for Accountants' Day.

## Formats

### Posts

4:5 (1080 × 1350), 1:1 (1200 × 1200) and 9:16 stories (1080 × 1920).

### E-mail and web

Covers 1640 × 624 and 1584 × 396, banners 1840 × 920, 1920 × 440 and 1920 × 800, footer 2300 × 400.

## Creative system

### Templates

Carousels with a cover and numbered slides - easy to read even when scrolling fast.

### Series

Themed series, e.g. about AI in accounting, in a single graphic layout.

### Brand

Orange, black and the Roboto typeface in line with the AKK brand book.

## Summary

Consistent templates for posts, carousels and stories built a recognisable AKK profile from the very first post, and I carried the same rules over to covers, banners and footers so the brand looks the same at every touchpoint.
