## Design concept

The design focuses on **movement, energy and wellness**, using flowing shapes and a dynamic logo that symbolise the path to recovery and balance. The colour palette blends calming blues, purples and greens - evoking trust, healing and vitality.

Minimalist typography keeps the text readable and matches the brand's professional yet friendly image.

## Layout & structure

### Front cover

Features the Coremotion logo, the tagline _“Pain-Free Living”_ and a clean, modern layout that immediately establishes the brand identity.

### Inside panels

Contain detailed information about the clinic's services, treatment methods and philosophy. Clear headings and short descriptions make the content easy to navigate.

### Back panel

Provides contact details, opening hours and social media links for quick access to information.

## Services

The brochure presents a wide range of physiotherapy treatments, including:

- Orthopaedic rehabilitation
- Sports physiotherapy
- Manual therapy
- Paediatric and neurological physiotherapy
- Therapeutic massage
- Electrotherapy and ultrasound therapy
- Urogynaecological physiotherapy

## Brand message

A key element of the design is the motivational line: **“Your Journey to Wellness Starts Here.”**

This message reinforces Coremotion's commitment to personalised care and to helping patients recover full fitness.

## Outcome

The final brochure combines **professional presentation** with **warm, inviting aesthetics**. It communicates trust, expertise and the holistic values of the Coremotion Physiotherapy brand. The result is a cohesive print piece that strengthens brand recognition and patient engagement.
