## Design Philosophy

The studio’s motto- **“From Concept to Visual Impact”**\- reflects its belief that effective design is a balance of **strategy, aesthetics, and purpose**.  
 KVISUAL focuses on creating visuals that are **minimal**, **memorable**, and **meaningful**.

### Typography & Style

- **Main typeface:** Futura - clean, functional, and ideal for modern communication.
- **Graphic style:** minimalism, thoughtful use of negative space, and contemporary dynamics.
- **Key techniques:** paper-texture effects, layered compositions, geometric forms, and creative collage layouts.
