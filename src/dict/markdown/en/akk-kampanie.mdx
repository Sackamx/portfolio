## My scope

I designed the creatives for Akademia Kształcenia Kadr's Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns. For each offer I prepared several message directions - financial benefit, leisure, convenient learning, help with paperwork - and carried each of them over to a full set of formats for different placements.

## Channels and goals

### Meta Ads

Residential courses subsidised up to 90%, corporate training and seasonal promotions, e.g. Accountants' Day and HR Day.

### Google Ads

Performance Max: accounting courses subsidised from PLN 5,000 to 15,000 and help with the job centre funding application.

## Formats

### Feed

1:1 (1080 × 1080) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350).

### Stories and reels

9:16 (1080 × 1920).

### Display and link

1.91:1 (1200 × 628).

## Creative system

### One layout

Each campaign shares one layout that I carry over to every format without losing legibility.

### Variants

Several versions of the creative per campaign, to test which graphic performs best.

### Series

One offer, several headlines and photos - e.g. 6 headlines for the corporate training campaign in a single layout.

### Brand

Orange, black and the Roboto typeface in line with the AKK brand book.

## Summary

The AKK creatives pair a concrete benefit - funding, an amount, the course location - with a clear call to action. Several message directions in the same formats show which headline and photo best catch the attention of accountants and HR specialists.
