## My scope

I designed the brand book from scratch: the chapter structure, page layouts, rule descriptions, clear-space diagrams, examples of correct and incorrect use, and branding visualisations. The Wektor Wiedzy logo is not my design - I rebuilt it as a vector so it can be scaled freely and used across all materials.

## Purpose of the book

The book sets out how Wektor Wiedzy's visual identity is used and the brand's communication standards. It is meant to build a consistent, professional and recognisable image across every customer touchpoint, based on the brand's values: growth, practical knowledge, professionalism and a partnership approach.

## Logo

### Symbol

A mark suggesting the letter "W" - the brand's visual anchor.

### Logotype

The name "Wektor Wiedzy" set in Niveau Grotesk.

### Slogan

"Miejsce Twojego rozwoju." (The place for your growth) - an inseparable part of the full logo.

### Variants

Horizontal (primary) and vertical, for when width is limited.

## Usage rules

### Clear space

A module defined by the gap between the symbol and the logotype - no text or photo details in this zone.

### Logo colour

Full colour on light backgrounds, white on dark, blue and photo backgrounds.

### Monochrome

Black only for black-and-white print, engraving, embossing and stamps.

### Symbol alone

Favicons, profile pictures, watermarks - once the brand has been introduced.

### Mistakes

No distortion, rotation, shadows, outlines or off-palette colours.

## Colour and typography

### Primary colour

Deep blue #0033A1 with tints - professionalism, trust and stability.

### Secondary colour

Orange #FF9D0A with tints - used sparingly, for CTA buttons and highlights.

### Typography

Montserrat in Black, Bold, Medium, Regular and Light weights.

## Summary

The book gathers all of Wektor Wiedzy's identity rules in one place, so every new brand material - from banners and social media to print - follows the same rules.
