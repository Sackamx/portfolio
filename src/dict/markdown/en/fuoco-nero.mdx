## Project objective

The goal was to design a website that combines a **sophisticated look** with a **clear and intuitive structure**, reflecting the brand’s character and strengthening its position in the premium segment.

## Concept & aesthetic

The website uses a **dark colour palette**, subtle typography and delicate copper or burnt-orange accents that symbolise the warmth of roasted beans. Large, atmospheric coffee photos and minimalist compositions create a mood of elegance and focus on the product.

## Key design elements

- Hero section - a bold coffee cup visual supporting the headline “Roasted to Perfection, Designed for Connoisseurs”.
- Timeline - the brand’s story, from tradition and growth to today’s quality.
- Featured Roasts section - selected products in a minimalist, prominent layout.
- Testimonials from experts and baristas - building the brand’s credibility and professionalism.
- Coffee subscription module - encouraging direct contact and recurring orders.

## Typography & graphic style

### Typography

Elegant and readable, built on weight contrast that creates hierarchy and highlights the luxurious character.

### Graphic style

Dark backgrounds, copper accents, generous margins and precise typography give the site a modern, refined look.

### Photography

High-quality, studio-style product photos - full of depth, light and shadow.

## User Experience

The website was designed to:

- guide users naturally from section to section,
- highlight the most important content through minimalism,
- present products in an elegant and sensory way.

## Accessibility & responsiveness

### Contrast

All text, including the form placeholders, meets the WCAG AA contrast standard.

### Content

Three coffees with their own tasting notes and the brand's history shown on a timeline.

### Responsiveness

Tablet and mobile versions: a product carousel, a vertical timeline and full-width buttons.

## Target audience

The project is aimed at people who value **high quality**, **premium design** and an **exceptional coffee experience**. It appeals both to connoisseurs and to customers who choose exclusive lifestyle products.

## Summary

The Fuoco Nero website combines **elegance, functionality and emotion**. It builds a luxurious brand image and offers an immersive experience that reflects the atmosphere of premium coffee - intense, refined and made with passion.
