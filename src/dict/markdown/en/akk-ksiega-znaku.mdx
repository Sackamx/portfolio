## My scope

I designed the brand book from scratch: the chapter structure, page layouts, rule descriptions, the clear-space diagram, examples of correct and incorrect use, and branding visualisations. The AKK logo is not my design - I rebuilt it as a vector so it can be scaled freely and used across all materials.

## Purpose of the book

The book sets out how Akademia Kształcenia Kadr's visual identity is used and the brand's communication standards. It is meant to build a consistent, professional and recognisable image of the training company across every customer touchpoint, based on its values: a practical approach, up-to-date knowledge, growth and professionalism.

## Logo

### Acronym

AKK as the main part of the logo - the last letter drawn as a simplified symbol.

### Symbol

A dynamic mark suggesting growth and the brand's energy.

### Logotype

The full name "Akademia Kształcenia Kadr" below the acronym.

## Usage rules

### Clear space

A module equal to 1/2 of the symbol's height - no text, symbols or background details in this zone.

### Logo colour

Full colour on light backgrounds, white on dark and photo backgrounds.

### Monochrome

Black or white for black-and-white print, engraving, embossing and stamps.

### Symbol alone

Favicons, profile pictures, watermarks - once the brand has been introduced.

### Mistakes

No distortion, rotation, shadows, outlines or off-palette colours.

## Colour and typography

### Black and grey

#122231 and #676767 - the image of an expert, stable brand.

### Typography

Roboto in Black, Bold, Medium, Regular and Light weights.

## Summary

The book gathers all of AKK's identity rules in one place, so every new brand material - from campaigns and social media to print - follows the same rules.
