## Project Overview

The _Wasabi_ restaurant menu design combines **modern minimalism** with **traditional Japanese aesthetics**. The goal was to create a visual identity that feels both **elegant and authentic**, reflecting the fusion of Japanese and Asian cuisine.

## Design Concept

The layout is **clean and well-organized**, featuring clearly defined sections such as _Starters_, _Soups_, _Noodles & Rice_, _Main Courses_, and _Drinks_.  
Soft **beige tones** paired with **green and red accents** create a natural, balanced look that aligns with the restaurant’s identity.

## Visual Elements

### Illustrations and Icons

Hand-drawn illustrations of ingredients and traditional food items add a **personal, artisanal touch** to the design, enhancing the overall warmth and authenticity.

### Typography

The logo features a **bold geometric font**, contrasted with a **clean serif typeface** for the menu text.  
Japanese calligraphy and a **large red circle**—a nod to the Japanese flag—serve as subtle cultural references that reinforce brand identity.

### Presentation

The menu is presented in a **folded mockup**, displayed on a minimalist two-tone background. This setup highlights the **professional and contemporary character** of the project while maintaining a handcrafted aesthetic.

## Summary

This design successfully merges **clarity, culture, and creativity** into a cohesive visual experience. The _Wasabi_ menu embodies simplicity, tradition, and modern sophistication—perfectly suited for a Japanese-Asian fusion restaurant.
