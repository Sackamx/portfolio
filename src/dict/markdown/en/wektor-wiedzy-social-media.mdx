## My scope

I designed graphics for Wektor Wiedzy's organic communication: post templates, event announcements and expert posts, as well as e-mail footers with current promotions and website banners. I set up recurring content as templates in which only the topic, date and host change.

## Channels and goals

### Organic

Announcements of free webinars, training and courses, event schedules, "Morning Coffee with Wektor Wiedzy" and expert posts from the blog.

### E-mail and web

E-mail footers with promotions and discount codes, and website banners, e.g. "September full of knowledge" and a tax conference in four cities.

## Formats

### Posts

1:1 (1350 × 1350) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350).

### E-mail and web

E-mail footers 1200 × 300, 2300 × 400 and 2400 × 600, website banners 1920 × 800.

## Creative system

### Templates

Fixed layouts for webinars, training, courses and schedules - you only swap the content.

### Series

Light and dark versions of the same template, so the profile never looks monotonous.

### Brand

Colours, the Montserrat typeface and graphic elements in line with the Wektor Wiedzy brand book.

## Summary

Templates and consistent rules make Wektor Wiedzy's communication look equally professional on social media, in the inbox and on the website, and new materials take less time to prepare.
