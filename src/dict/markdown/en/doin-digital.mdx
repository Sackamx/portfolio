## My scope

I co-founded Doin Digital, and the brand's content was made as a team. I recorded and published the reels in which I appear on camera, applied revisions to the designs and was responsible for the copywriting - editing the text of posts, carousels and ads.

## How it was made

### Skill

First we built a skill for AI - a record of the brand rules: colours, typefaces, layouts and tone of voice.

### AI designs

Based on it, AI produced the first versions of graphics, carousels and ads.

### Revisions

I refined the designs: applied revisions and kept everything consistent and legible.

### Copywriting

I edited the copy - headlines, slogans and calls to action - so that they sounded like the brand.

## Formats

### Reels

9:16 (1080 × 1920).

### Posts and carousels

4:5 (1080 × 1350).

### Ads

1:1 (1200 × 1200), 9:16 (1200 × 1920) and 1.91:1 (1200 × 628).

## Creative system

### Templates

Recurring post layouts, e.g. receipt, poll and clipping.

### Series

Educational carousels about selling courses and a collage series.

### Brand

Violet with lime, pink and orange - a bold palette that stands out in the feed.

## Summary

Combining AI with hands-on editing made it possible to create a lot of consistent content quickly, and the reels I appear in gave the brand a recognisable face.
