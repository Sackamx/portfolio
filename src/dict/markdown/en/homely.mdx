## Project objective

To design a clear landing page for a real estate agency that leads from searching for a home, through featured listings, to contacting the team - and works equally well on desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Process

### Sketch

The first page layout and the order of sections.

### Wireframes & UI kit

Grey mock-ups in three sizes and a UI kit concept - buttons, listing cards, testimonials and team cards.

### Responsive GUI

The finished interface with colours and photos, adapted to 1440, 834 and 430 px widths, plus a Projects subpage.

## Page sections

- Hero with the headline “We will help you to find your perfect home” and a search bar
- Popular Properties - a carousel of featured listings
- Claim your best living with us
- Client testimonials
- Our Team
- Blog articles
- “Get started with Homely” call to action and footer

## Graphic style

A light, calm interface with blue as the brand colour and orange action buttons. Large photos of homes and rounded cards create a friendly character that builds trust.

## Accessibility & UX

### Contrast

Orange buttons use dark text and the blue CTA band uses a darker shade - all text meets WCAG AA, while the buttons previously had only 2.6:1.

### Readability

Text is at least 12 px on tablet and at least 16 px on desktop and mobile.

### Content

Every listing, article and section has its own copy instead of repeated placeholder text.
