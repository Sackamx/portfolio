## My scope

In Illustrator I designed two PLN 1,000 gift vouchers and a branded photo wall for Wektor Wiedzy. All of them were printed and used at residential training events.

## Use

The vouchers were a prize for training participants - to spend on courses, online training or the training platform. The wall was the backdrop for souvenir photos that participants posted on social media, tagging Wektor Wiedzy.

## Designs

### Universal voucher

A large amount in the brand orange, a red ribbon and the contact details of the training coordinators.

### Course voucher

A version for a specific course - "Full delegation" - with a photo from the training room.

### Photo wall

A large Wektor Wiedzy logo with the slogan and a repeated logotype pattern on a navy background - legible in every photo.

### Brand

Colours, typeface and logo in line with the Wektor Wiedzy brand book.

## Summary

The designs proved themselves in practice: the vouchers appeared in stories from the training events and the wall was the backdrop of participants' photos, so the brand was visible beyond the event itself.
