## Concept

An annual **festival of human connection** - a bit like networking, but focused on personal relationships. An event where **you come not for a product, but for a companion**. Before entering, every participant fills in a short form: what they want to do and who they want to do it with. The festival is divided into zones, so everyone can find the right space for themselves.

## Event zones

### Creative Zone

Musicians, painters, writers and filmmakers looking for co-creators.

### Adventure Zone

Organisers of expeditions, climbs and trips looking for companions.

### Ideas Zone

Start-ups, social projects and shared businesses.

### Meeting Zone

Simply meeting someone new - for a coffee, a walk or a chat.

### Learning Zone

Want to teach a language, dance or code? Looking for a teacher or a student.

### Local Zone

City projects - local initiatives, volunteering and neighbourhood life.

## Logo

The logo is the core of the festival identity. The symbol of two intersecting circles is inspired by a **Venn diagram** - **two people and a shared space**. The mark combines a symbol and a wordmark; the symbol can also stand alone, for example as an app icon or favicon.

## Colours & typography

### Primary colours

Light Violet #9B7FE8 and Mint #6ECFB5 - the logo colours, which must not be modified.

### Secondary colours

Dark Violet #1A1028, Violet #7C5CBF, Dark Mint #1E9E75 and Modern Gold #EF9F27.

### Typography

Roboto across all materials; Rubik Spray Paint as the only accent, in the “Warsaw 2026” tagline.

## Applications

The identity includes:

- a brand book with logo construction, clear space and usage rules
- a poster with the festival programme, a roll-up and a banner
- an Instagram carousel, a festival wristband and tote bags
- a website in desktop and mobile versions
