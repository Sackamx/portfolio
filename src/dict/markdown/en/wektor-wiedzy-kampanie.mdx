## My scope

I designed the creatives for Wektor Wiedzy's Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns. I prepared every campaign as a full set of formats for different placements - feed, stories, display network - often in several design variants so there was something to test.

## Channels and goals

### Meta Ads

Sign-ups for free webinars, courses subsidised up to 90%, English courses and seasonal promotions, e.g. a PLN 500 gift for Accountants' Day and HR Day.

### Google Ads

Performance Max and sales campaigns: Black Weeks with a discount code, an education voucher of up to PLN 600, courses for accountants and HR specialists.

## Formats

### Feed

1:1 (1200 × 1200 or 1080 × 1080) and 4:5 (1080 × 1350).

### Stories and reels

9:16 (1080 × 1920).

### Display and link

1.91:1 (1200 × 628).

## Creative system

### One layout

Each campaign shares one layout that I carry over to every format without losing legibility.

### Variants

Several versions of the creative per campaign, to test which graphic performs best.

### Series

An expert webinar series: the same template, with a new topic, date and host.

### Brand

Colours, the Montserrat typeface and graphic elements in line with the Wektor Wiedzy brand book.

## Summary

I designed the graphics around the campaign goal and placement: the message has to read well in a small preview and the call-to-action button has to stand out straight away. A consistent system of formats and variants makes it quick to launch new campaigns and test which creatives work best.
